### Description

- Built an algorithm to estimate a region’s **greenery score** from longitude/latitude and radius, using OpenStreetMap data for land-use signals.
- Integrated the **OpenStreetMap API** to compute city-level scores (e.g., Delhi greenery score **~24%**) and validate outputs with spot checks.
- Analyzed **NASA MODIS fire** datasets and prototyped a region-level fire-count forecasting workflow for risk monitoring.
- Demonstrated how proactive fire-risk alerts could reduce losses for fire-prone operations (insurance, logistics, and land-heavy businesses).
